chapter four

A letter for 21.

My love,

I wish I could be beside you today. I wish I could see your face when you wake up, annoy you all day, steal a birthday hug and make you laugh until you tell me to stop.

I'm sorry I couldn't be there at midnight. I wanted to be. But even when I couldn't reach you, you were still the person in my thoughts when the clock passed 12.

I hope 21 is kind to you. I hope you grow into everything you've dreamed of becoming, and I hope you never forget how deeply you are loved.

Distance can steal a midnight from me. It cannot steal my love for you.

So here's to you, baby. To 21. To every stupid joke, every late-night conversation, every “I miss you”, every kiss sent through a screen — and to all the birthdays I hope I get to celebrate beside you someday.

Happy 21st, my thango. ❤️
